# Choosing Speakers

Play on the Mac's outputs, Bluetooth speakers, AirPlay receivers or Spotify Connect devices.

## Overview

Open the speaker picker with **D**, the speaker button in the toolbar or Card layout, or **Choose Speaker…** in the
menu. It has four sections.

![The speaker picker with This Mac, Bluetooth, AirPlay and Spotify Connect sections.](speakers)

## This Mac

The Mac's audio outputs: built-in speakers, connected Bluetooth devices, USB audio and display audio. Picking one
makes it the Mac's default output, which Spotify follows. If Spotify was playing on another device, Dotify also moves
playback back to this Mac.

Virtual drivers such as BlackHole, Teams and remote-desktop audio are hidden under **More**.

## Bluetooth · Not Connected

Paired speakers and headphones that aren't connected. Click one to connect it and switch sound to it. Connected
Bluetooth devices appear under **This Mac**.

## AirPlay

AirPlay receivers on your network. Sonos speakers are labelled. macOS doesn't let apps choose an AirPlay target, so
clicking one opens Sound settings, where it's one click under **Output**. Once chosen it appears under **This Mac**.

## Spotify Connect

Network speakers, TVs, phones and other devices running Spotify. Click one to move playback there. The active device
gets its own volume slider. The list refreshes every five seconds while the picker is open.

This section needs a Spotify connection with playback permissions, and Spotify Premium. See <doc:ConnectingSpotify>.

## Sonos speakers

Spotify's Web API usually leaves Sonos speakers out of its device list, or marks them as uncontrollable. Spotify has
acknowledged this for years. Ways to reach a Sonos speaker from Dotify:

- **AirPlay**: pick it in the AirPlay section, then select it in Sound settings.
- **Bluetooth**: speakers with Bluetooth, such as the Sonos Move, can be paired with the Mac and then connected in one
  click from the Bluetooth section.
