# Search and Recently Played

Find songs, playlists and podcasts, or jump back to something you played.

## Overview

Open the window with **F** (or **/**) in the player, the search button in the toolbar, or
**Search & Recently Played…** in the menu.

With the search box empty, it shows **Recently Played**, newest first, with how long ago each item played.

![Recently Played: songs, playlists and podcast episodes, newest first, with times like 2h and 1d.](search-recents)

## Search

Type to search Spotify. Results arrive as you type and are grouped into **Songs**, **Playlists** and **Podcasts**.
Podcasts include both shows and episodes; picking a show plays its newest episode.

Search needs a Spotify connection. See <doc:ConnectingSpotify>.

## Filters

The **Songs**, **Playlists** and **Podcasts** chips are multi-select and apply to both search and Recently Played.
Option-click a chip to show only that kind. At least one stays on, and your choice is remembered.

![Search for "lantern" with Songs and Podcasts selected and Playlists off.](search-results)

## Keyboard

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| ↑ / ↓ | Move the selection |
| Return | Play the selection and close the window |
| Esc | Clear the search, or close the window |

Clicking a row plays it and keeps the window open, so you can keep browsing.

## Where Recently Played comes from

- **Spotify's history**: your last 50 songs, plus the playlists you played them from. Needs a Spotify connection.
- **Dotify's own history**: everything Dotify saw play, up to 150 items, stored on your Mac. Works without
  connecting. Spotify's history doesn't include podcasts, so podcast episodes only appear if they played while
  Dotify was running.
